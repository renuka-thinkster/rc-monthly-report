💰 Employee Incentive Sheet

Creator adds employees, sets Leave days, marks Absent (+ Remark), and can set a Fix Incentive (overrides all calculation). Manager enters Total Incentive pool & %, and can round-off via Mgr Override.
Incentive = Total × % · Per Day = Incentive ÷ days in month · Paid Days = days − Leave · Pro-rated = Per Day × Paid Days.
Enter each employee's full-month amount in Actual Incentive. Per Day = that amount ÷ Days in month, and Leave Days are deducted at that rate. Use Fix Incentive for an exact amount with no deduction. Absent is only a marker — for a whole month off set Leave Days = days in month. Once ✅ Approved by an admin, the sheet is locked — only an admin can modify it.
